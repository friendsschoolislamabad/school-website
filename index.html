About us

Happy, secure children learn best

Friends School is a preschool and activity center for children aged 12 months to 5+ years. Everything we do starts from one belief: children thrive when they feel safe, known and happy.

Each day balances early literacy and numeracy with play, stories, art and time outdoors. Emotional wellbeing is part of every day, not a separate subject: children learn to name their feelings, solve problems together and treat one another with kindness. Our aim is simple: children who move on to big school confident, curious and ready to learn.

  • Small groups, so every child gets real attention
  • Learning through play, stories, art and the outdoors
  • Wellbeing built into the day, guided by a trained therapist

Our approach

Happy children make brilliant learners

Four ideas guide everything we do, from how we set up a classroom to how we talk with your child at the gate.

1

Learning through play

Play is serious work for young children. Our classrooms are arranged into learning corners where children build, pretend, count, read and create.

2

Feelings matter

With a therapist at the heart of the school, we help children recognise and manage big emotions, build resilience and form healthy friendships.

3

Small groups, big attention

Low teacher-to-child ratios mean we notice the little things: who needs a challenge, who needs encouragement, and who needs a quiet moment.

4

Parents as partners

You know your child best. Regular updates, open conversations and parent sessions keep home and school growing in the same direction.
